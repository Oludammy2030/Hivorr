# AGENT.md — Hivorr AI Development & Planning Directive

## Core Operational Directives
* **Strictly Bounded Scope:** Only act upon the specific tasks, prompts, or files explicitly provided in the current conversation. Do not assume, search for, or reference unprovided roadmaps, external files, or hidden plans.
* **Separation of Concerns:** Keep business logic strictly separate from presentation UI. Never hardcode platform logic or financial rules inside client presentation code.
* **Proprietary Logic Protection (Server-Side Enforcement):** Never embed proprietary business logic, pricing formulas, matching algorithms, or operational rules inside the client application code. All core business intelligence must remain hidden and execute securely on the server-side to prevent reverse-engineering or decompilation.
* **Deterministic Core Supremacy:** Internal platform recommendation algorithms and deterministic decision engines make all core operational, financial, search ranking, and routing decisions. The AI never overrides them.
* **Code Standards:** Write modular, reusable, clean, null-safe Dart/Flutter code. Always check and reuse existing utilities before creating new components.
* **Clarification First:** Always ask for clarification instead of making assumptions regarding business logic or feature requirements.

## Non-Negotiable System & Security Guardrails
* **Rule 1 (Engine vs. AI):** Core platform intelligence, routing, and payouts are handled by dedicated system engines. The AI handles conversational wrappers and drafting helpers only; it never decides marketplace rankings or financial splits.
* **Rule 2 (Dual Taxonomy & Verification):** Two taxonomies govern the platform: Profession taxonomy (Industry $\rightarrow$ Profession, trade-gated) and Product taxonomy for the Local Market (vertical $\rightarrow$ category $\rightarrow$ subcategory $\rightarrow$ product type $\rightarrow$ attributes, merchant/store catalog-gated, admin-configurable). Anyone who wants to sell, offer a professional service, or provide logistics must undergo verification with a document or proof of trade: professionals submit identity + mandatory trade proof, sellers submit store profile + identity + mandatory proof-of-trade/store document, logistics providers submit identity + KYC + mandatory vehicle/logistics proof. Unverified participants receive dashboard access immediately, but gated writes stay locked until verification is `APPROVED`: job bidding/accepting for services (`tradeVerificationStatus == APPROVED`), product publishing for sellers (seller verification `APPROVED`), dispatch acceptance for logistics (rider verification `APPROVED` — no dispatch without approval).
* **Rule 3 (Financial Guardrails & KYC-Gated Access):** Deposit webhooks enforce `Payer Name == Entity Profile Name`. Payouts are restricted to bound, pre-verified bank accounts with strict KYC-driven cashout limits. Access to some areas is subject to KYC: withdrawals, payouts, conversions, and higher transaction limits require a verified tier (`tier_1` and above; `tier_0` Unverified carries zero/all-zero limits). Earning activities (selling, offering services for paid work, logistics dispatch) require the applicable verification lane plus the required KYC tier; gated writes stay locked until both are satisfied.
* **Rule 4 (Database-First Logic & Zero-Trust Client):** Sensitive financial calculations, pricing logic, escrow splitting, and cryptographic verification checks must **never** reside inside the client app code. All critical state changes must execute server-side via PostgreSQL Stored Procedures (RPC) protected by Row-Level Security (RLS).
* **Rule 5 (Visual Identity & Design Tokens):** All UI MUST consume `AppTheme` tokens (`ColorScheme` + `AppThemeExtension` for color, `TextTheme` for type, `assets/images/logo*.svg` for the brand mark) defined in `documents/Context/VISUAL-IDENTITY.md` (what Hivorr looks like). How Flutter/Dart implements that quality — constraints, rebuilds, lazy/slivers, images, keyboard, `const`, motion consumption — follows `documents/Context/FLUTTER-UI-IMPLEMENTATION-RULES.md`. Never hardcode `Colors.*`/raw hex in widgets, never set `fontFamily` per-widget, and never introduce a third brand hue. `VISUAL-IDENTITY.md` is the project-wide source of truth across EP-01 → EP-08; any UI task that hardcodes a color or font fails its Definition of Done. Role accents: Explore/hire uses Client blue `#2D3FE7`, Earn/offer uses Professional green `#16A34A`, Admin uses `#1A2AD4` with `#8B5CF6` accent. There is no Both account identity.
* **Rule 6 (Unified Account & Activities):** One Hivorr account activates multiple activities over time: Explore (buy, hire, discover) and Earn (sell, offer services, provide logistics). Activities are capabilities, not account types. The Both value is permanently removed. Admin and Super Admin are privileged platform roles, never selectable through registration or activity selection. Ubiquitous language is merchant / rider / store / product where applicable; do not invent Client / Professional / Both synonyms.
