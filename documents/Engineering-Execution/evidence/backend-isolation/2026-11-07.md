## Controls added

- `server/src/config.ts` requires `BACKEND_ENVIRONMENT` and `SUPABASE_PROJECT_REF` in staging and
  production, checks that they match `HIVORR_ENV`, and rejects a `DATABASE_URL` that does not belong
  to the declared project.
- `scripts/verify-backend-target.mjs` performs the deployment preflight and prints only the
  environment and public project reference.
- `tests/backend-target.test.ts` covers matching, cross-environment, shared-project, and mismatched
  database URL cases.
