  directory, and keeps the Linux-only architecture policy inherited from EP-00.
- Seven workspace packages exist with unique names and resolvable entries: `@hivorr/shared`,

  `@hivorr/app-staff`, `@hivorr/app-partner`. The root package remains the Figma Make web app.

  through their `exports`; `tsc --noEmit` exits 0.

  rules, and fails on a seeded violation.
- `pnpm build:all` produces the main bundle and the three privileged bundles; `pnpm build:server`
  produces the API/worker build.
- `src/app/App.tsx` and `src/styles/theme.css` are unchanged against `v0.0.1-bootstrap`.
